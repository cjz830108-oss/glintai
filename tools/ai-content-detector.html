Frequently asked questions

Is this AI detector accurate?

It's a fast heuristic, not a lab-grade classifier. Treat the score as a rough signal; for high-stakes decisions use a paid detector.

Do you upload my text?

No. Scanning happens locally in your browser.

What is burstiness and why does it matter?

Burstiness is how much sentence length varies. Human writing swings between short and long sentences; AI text tends to be uniform. Low burstiness raises the AI score.
