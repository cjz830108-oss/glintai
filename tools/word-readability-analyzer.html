Frequently asked questions

What is a good reading ease score?

On the Flesch scale, 60–70 is 'standard' and readable for most adults, 70–90 is 'easy'. Aim for 60+ for general web and SEO copy.

Does the readability analyzer store my text?

No. Analysis happens in your browser; nothing is uploaded or saved.

Why does readability matter for SEO?

Readable pages keep visitors engaged and reduce bounce rate. Clear copy also tends to earn more featured snippets and longer dwell time.
