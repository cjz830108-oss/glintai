Frequently asked questions

Is my image uploaded?

No. The model runs in your browser; the image never leaves your device.

Why does the first run take longer?

The model (~40MB) downloads once and is cached by your browser for instant reuse.

What format do I get?

Output is a transparent PNG you can drop into any design tool.
