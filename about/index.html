What is Glint AI?

Glint AI is a collection of 16 free, browser-based AI tools for the work creators and marketers do every day: summarizing, rewriting, checking grammar, formatting JSON, generating titles and hashtags, previewing search snippets, and more. The free tools run entirely in your browser — your text never leaves your device, and there is no account required to use them.

Why we built it

Most "free" AI tools quietly upload your text to a server, lock core features behind a login, or bury you in tracking cookies. We took the opposite stance: privacy-first by default, no signup walls on free tools, and a clean, fast interface. When a tool needs more power, Pro unlocks higher usage — it never takes away what was free.

How we make money (and stay free)

Glint AI is supported by four transparent channels: Pro subscriptions (PayPal, $9.99/mo or $79/yr) for higher usage limits; affiliate recommendations on our AI Tool Reviews page (we only list tools we'd genuinely recommend); ads shown as clearly-labeled placeholders; and a future Chrome extension that drives users back to the hub. We do not sell your data, and we do not place cross-site tracking cookies.
