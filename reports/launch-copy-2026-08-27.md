## 2. Indie Hackers

### Post title
**I built 16 free, privacy-first AI tools — here's the model (and the traffic problem)**

### Body
> **What it is**
> Glint AI is a collection of 16 free AI tools for writers, developers, and marketers — summarizer, humanizer, grammar checker, JSON formatter, markdown converter, password generator, YouTube title generator, and more. The twist: everything runs **in the browser**, so user text is never uploaded. No account needed to use any tool.
>
> **Why I built it**
> I was tired of "free" tools that quietly harvested whatever I pasted in. For client work and API debugging that's a real risk. A client-side toolkit removes it. The privacy promise is also the marketing: "we can't leak what we never receive."
>
> **The business model**
> - Free, no signup, for all 16 tools.
> - Pro at $9/mo — batch processing, higher limits, no ads.
> - Team at $29/mo — shared seats for small teams.
> - Payments via PayPal Subscriptions; auth via Supabase.
>
> **The honest part — traffic**
> We're ~3 weeks old. Google has indexed ~45 pages but we're not ranking yet: new domain, zero backlinks, and "AI tools" is brutally competitive. The plan is long-tail SEO (e.g. "private AI detector no upload," "free JSON formatter no signup") plus this kind of community posting instead of paid ads.
>
> **What I'd love from you**
> - Which tool should we build next?
> - If you've grown a tool site from zero, what actually moved the needle for you?
>
> Site: https://glintai.tools — would genuinely appreciate a click and a gut reaction.

### Reply templates
- **On "how do you monetize free?"** → "Pro/Team tiers via PayPal. Free stays free because the privacy story only works if people actually use it."
- **On "client-side = limited?"** → "Correct for heavy ML. We keep ML light/optional and do the rest in JS. Good enough for 90% of daily tasks, and that's the point."
- **On "SEO takes months"** → "Yes — that's why launch posts like this and a few backlinks are the near-term lever. Happy to report back in 90 days."
