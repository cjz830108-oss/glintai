Why a generic chatbot is a weak story generator

Ask a model for "a story" and you usually get a tidy parable with no engine behind it. Real stories need structure.

💡

No narrative engine

A chatbot optimizes the sentence in front of it. It has no sense of rising tension, so the middle sags and the ending feels random rather than earned.

👨

Flat, interchangeable people

Without a character system, everyone sounds like the same narrator wearing a different name. The drama that comes from clashing wants is missing.

💦

Dead clues, no payoff

It will drop a mysterious object in paragraph one and never mention it again. A story needs planted questions to resolve, not loose sparks.
