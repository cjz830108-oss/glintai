The real problem with AI and fiction

Tools are smart sentence by sentence and dumb book by book. That gap is where manuscripts fall apart.

📕

It forgets the rules of your world

Magic systems, currency, and geography slip within a few chapters because the model has no persistent place to store them. Readers who care about logic notice first.

💬

Voice drifts chapter to chapter

Without a stored character voice, dialogue flattens into one polite narrator. Distinct people stop sounding distinct, and the fiction loses its texture.

⏳

Promises to the reader go unkept

A mystery set up in act one should resolve by act three. Raw generation loses the thread, leaving dangling questions that frustrate finishing readers.
